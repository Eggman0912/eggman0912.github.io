Why Do People Underestimate Making Games?


"I want to make a game."

Many people who say this probably think something like this first: write a few scripts, add a character, build a map, and the game is done. From the outside, it really looks that simple. After all, you only see a moving character and some working mechanics on the screen. But making a game that feels like a real game needs much more work than people think.
